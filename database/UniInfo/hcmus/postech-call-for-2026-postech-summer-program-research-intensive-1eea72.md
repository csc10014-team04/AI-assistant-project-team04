25/12/2025 28/12/2025

## [POSTECH] Call for 2026 POSTECH Summer Program (Research Intensive)
Phòng Quan hệ Đối ngoại xin chuyển tiếp thông tin t ừ Đại học Khoa học và Công nghệ Pohang Hàn Quốc (POSTECH) về Chương trình hè nghiên cứu POSTECH 2026.
Thời gian: 06/06 – 14/08/2026 (6 tuần)
Thông tin chi tiết và link đăng ký vui lòng xem tại email và file thông tin đính kèm. 2026 POSTECH Summer Program Brochure
Về việc đề cử sinh viên tham dự, Phòng QHĐN dự kiến sẽ đề cử 02 sinh viên cho chương trình. Sinh viên mong muốn được đề cử cần nộp hồ sơ bản cứng và bản mềm đến Phòng QHĐN (227 Nguyễn Văn Cừ, P. Chợ Quán, [email removed] ) đến hết ngày 16/01/2026 để được xem xét và hỗ trợ.
Quá thời hạn trên, sinh viên có thể tiếp tục đăng ký trực tiếp với POSTECH theo diện trả phí đến 15/02/2026.
We are excited to announce that application for the 2026 POSTECH Summer Program will soon open from Christmas!
This exclusive opportunity is designed for STEM-major students currently enrolled in oversea institutions.
All admitted students will receive a ROUND-TRIP FLIGHT TICKET from their home country to Korea.
Please encourage your students to apply and take advantage of this incredible experience at POSTECH this summer.
Program Overview ( Link )
- Period: 6 July – 14 August, 2026 (6 weeks)
- Eligibility: Undergraduate or Master’s student from oversea institutions
Exchange Track | Concurrent Track
Eligibility | Officially nominated from POSTECH Partner Institutions | Unable to be nominated by Partner Institution or from non-partner
Application Fee | Waived | 100,000 KRW
Program Fee | Waived | 1,500,000 KRW
* Students under Exchange Track will be counted for exchange quota (2 summer to 1 regular semester)
* POSTECH Partner Institution means Institution that have university level bilateral Student Exchange Agreement (SEA) with POSTECH
Program Structure & Outcome
l Research Intensive (min 20 hours per week) under POSTECH Advisor
l Social & Culture Program (once a week)
l Korean Language Course (optional, approx. 150 USD)
l Official Transcript with 1 POSTECH credit & Certificate of Participation
Application Guideline
l Application Period: December 25, 2025 – February 15, 2026
l Online Application: Link
l Open Advisor List: Link
l After submitting the online application, students should register IR Portal (Student-Mobility) and submit following documents.
I Certificate of Enrollment
I Official Academic Transcript
I Recommendation Letter
I Copy of Passport
I CV / Resume
I Cover Letter
l Please note that there is no separate nomination process required for exchange track. However, after submitting the documents, students should get confirmation from their home institution coordinator through our IR Portal system .
l There is no limit on the number of applicants per institution. Admission is competitive, and selection will be based on a thorough review of all applications worldwide. Once the student is selected and accepted the admission offer under exchange track, 2 PSP students will be counted as 1 semester academic exchange student .
If you have further inquiries, feel free to contact us at [email removed] .
